Privacy-Preserving Image and Text Classification

Python • Differential Privacy • Neural Networks • MNIST

Neural network training with Differential Privacy (DP) techniques to prevent dataset reconstruction and membership inference attacks.

  • Implemented DP-SGD with gradient clipping and noise injection, achieving ε=5.0 privacy guarantee while maintaining >95% accuracy on MNIST
  • Mathematically provable protection against model inversion and reconstruction attacks on training data
  • Demonstrated minimal accuracy trade-off: baseline >98% vs. DP-trained >95% with formal privacy guarantees
